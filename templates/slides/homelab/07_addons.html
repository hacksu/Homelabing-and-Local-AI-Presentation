07 - ADD-ONS

EXTENSIONS AND ADD-ONS

Storage

Add a NAS for external storage, or use the server's internal RAID controller.

Backups

RAID is not a backup. Follow 3-2-1:

  • 3 copies
  • 2 media types
  • 1 offsite

Me: setting up automatic backups of all my data to the NAS.

UPS

Power can go out at any moment. A UPS (battery backup) keeps the server up long enough to shut down non-essential VMs safely and keep essential ones running.

Me: Proxmox start/shutdown order decides what goes down first.
